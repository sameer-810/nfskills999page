Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

This policy explains how NFskills ("we", "us") handles your personal data when you use this website or register for the 10-Day Production Deployment Accelerator. By registering, you agree to the uses described here.

Information we collect

  • Details you enter: your full name, phone number and current location.
  • Payment records: the amount, date, status, transaction ID, order ID and invoice number.
  • Card, UPI and bank details: these are entered directly with Razorpay. We do not receive or store them.

How we use your information

  • To register you for the bootcamp and confirm your payment.
  • To create your invoice and let you download it again.
  • To add you to the course WhatsApp group and share class updates.
  • To answer your questions and provide support.
  • To keep accounting records and meet legal obligations.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with the services that run this registration process:

  • Razorpay processes payments and stores your order details.
  • Google stores our registration records in Google Sheets.
  • WhatsApp runs the course group. Other group members may be able to see your phone number.
  • Our website hosting provider runs this site.

We may also disclose information when the law requires it.

Information stored on your device

This site saves your invoice references in your browser so you can download invoices again from "Find my invoice". We do not use advertising or analytics cookies. Razorpay may use its own cookies during checkout.

How long we keep it

We keep your details for as long as needed to deliver the course, support you and meet accounting, tax and legal requirements.

Keeping it safe

Payments are verified on our server, and our keys are never exposed in the website. No online service can be completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your data.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal data, or withdraw your consent. We may need to keep some records where the law requires it. If you have a complaint about how we handle your data, contact us using the details below.

Children

Anyone under 18 should register only with the consent of a parent or guardian.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The latest version and its date will always appear on this page.
